CC BY 4.0: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. Full citations for
each UCI dataset, including the creators and DOI, are in
`manifests/<dataset>-audit-v1.json` and in the report's references.

**Changes made to the UCI data.** Every prepared CSV adds a `row_id` column
and applies no scaling or imputation. Beyond that:

- Airfoil Self-Noise: no other change.
- Real Estate Valuation: the transaction ID column `No` is removed.
- Parkinsons Telemonitoring: `subject#` is kept for grouping only, and
  `total_UPDRS` is not used as a feature; the target is `motor_UPDRS`.
- Forest Fires: month and day are one-hot coded, and the target is
  `log1p(area)`.
- Superconductivity: only `train.csv` is used. The pilot takes a fixed
  5,000-row subset; the full-data runs use all 21,263 rows.

Details are in each dataset's manifest (`manifests/<dataset>-v1.json`) and in
`../reports/external-dataset-preparation-audit-v1.md`. The raw UCI archives are
not committed;
`../src/fetch_uci_data.py` downloads them and checks them against the audited
SHA-256 values.
