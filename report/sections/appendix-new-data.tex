\section{New-data Provenance and Examples}
\label{app:new-data-provenance}

\textbf{Frozen preparation.}
Public-data audits retain the UCI dataset records, source citations, downloaded-file hashes and column roles.
Prepared public-data CSV files preserve source row order; the historical Superconductivity pilot selects a subset and sorts its sampled source indices before storage.
The full-data experiment retains all source rows and is not pooled with that pilot.
Forest Fires retains four exact duplicate rows and allows repeated spatial cells across row partitions.
Real Estate row partitions mix transaction dates and may share locations, so they do not represent future-period or location-disjoint prediction.

\textbf{Randomness and partitions.}
Repeated public-data row holdouts use sequential permutations from NumPy's legacy random generator (\texttt{RandomState}), seeded with 1, with inner-split seed $10000+k$ for zero-based repetition or fold index $k$, counted separately in each experiment.
Grouped Airfoil and Parkinsons use 5 shuffled partitions of sorted condition or subject identifiers with seed 1; inner validation holds out 20\% of outer-training groups with seed $10000+k$.
All variants reuse the saved indices, with stage-model seed $20000+k$ and refit-model seed $30000+k$.
For fresh synthetic replicate $r\in\{1,\ldots,10\}$, PCG64 generators use data seed $r$ and split seed $1000+r$.
A single permutation assigns the training, validation and test labels, shared across regimes.
Stage-model and refit-model seeds are $20000+100r$ and $30000+100r$, respectively.
Each synthetic manifest records its generator hash, data seed, split seed and generator type.
