\section{Replication Environment and Diagnostics}
\label{app:replication-environment}

\textbf{Recorded environments.}
The historical environment record lists Python~3.10.0, NGBoost~0.5.11.dev0 installed in editable mode from commit \texttt{6720340}, NumPy~2.2.6, pandas~2.3.3, SciPy~1.15.3 and scikit-learn~1.6.1.
It does not record a CPU model or numerical-library thread settings.
The controlled selector experiment records the same package versions on Linux~6.8.0-139-generic, x86-64, with glibc~2.39.
Before execution, \texttt{OMP\_NUM\_THREADS}, \texttt{OPENBLAS\_NUM\_THREADS}, \texttt{MKL\_NUM\_THREADS} and \texttt{NUMEXPR\_NUM\_THREADS} were each set to \texttt{1}.
These restrictions support reproducibility within the recorded environment; they do not establish identical arithmetic across platforms.

\textbf{Controlled split provenance.}
For zero-based repetition index $k$, the controlled selector experiment uses inner-split seed $10000+k$, independently of model fitting, seeds stage-selection models with $20000+k$ and refit models with $30000+k$, and preserves the resulting indices.
The separate Yacht Natural headroom diagnostic also freezes inner partitions with seed $10000+k$, seeds inner models with $20000+k$ and refit models with $30000+k$, and compares both caps on shared 4{,}000-stage fits.
Its NLL means are diagnostic results, not substitutes for the controlled selector experiment's means.

\textbf{Measures and dispersion.}
For observations $y_i$, predictive densities $p_i$ and predicted means $\hat\mu_i$,
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{NLL} &= -\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log p_i(y_i), \\
\mathrm{RMSE} &= \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\hat\mu_i)^2}.
\end{align*}
As a worked value, a Normal prediction with $y=\mu=0$ and $\sigma=0.1$ has NLL $\log(0.1\sqrt{2\pi})\approx-1.384$.
The original paper reports standard errors over its repetitions, whereas the released script prints population standard deviations.
Table~\ref{tab:replication-results} therefore carries the published standard errors only; adding local spread would make that cross-protocol table too wide to read, and the per-repetition values are kept in the run records.
