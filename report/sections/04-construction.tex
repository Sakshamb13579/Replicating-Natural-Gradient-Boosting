\section{Construction of New Data}
\label{sec:new-data}

Five existing public regression datasets outside the original benchmark test generalisation across domains.
Constructed data provides known conditional means and noise scales for directly checking uncertainty estimates.

\textbf{Public sources and preparation.}
Table~\ref{tab:new-data-overview} summarises the model inputs and outer partitions.
Airfoil Self-Noise contains wind-tunnel measurements predicting scaled sound-pressure level from frequency and four aerodynamic variables \cite{uci-airfoil}.\footnote{\url{https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/291/airfoil+self+noise}}
Its headerless tab-separated file was assigned descriptive column names.
Real Estate Valuation predicts unit-area house price from transaction date, house age, transport proximity, nearby stores and coordinates \cite{uci-real-estate}.\footnote{\url{https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/477/real+estate+valuation+data+set}}
The sole spreadsheet worksheet was loaded and its source index removed.
Parkinsons Telemonitoring predicts motor severity from voice measurements, age, sex and test time \cite{uci-parkinsons}.\footnote{\url{https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/189/parkinsons+telemonitoring}}
Subject identifiers define partitions only; the alternate total-severity outcome is excluded from predictors.

Forest Fires combines weather, spatial coordinates and calendar categories to predict burned area \cite{uci-forest-fires}.\footnote{\url{https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/162/forest+fires}}
Its month and day fields were expanded using fixed calendar categories, giving 29 numeric predictors, and the target became $\log(1+\mathrm{area})$.
The transform reduces skew but retains the mass at zero, so a Normal likelihood remains approximate; subsequent metrics are on the transformed scale.
Superconductivity uses the 81 numeric predictors in \texttt{train.csv} to predict critical temperature \cite{uci-superconductivity}.\footnote{\url{https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/464/superconductivty+data}}
The chemical-formula strings in its companion file are not model inputs.
All 21{,}263 rows are used here; an earlier 5{,}000-row subset remains separate pilot evidence.

\textbf{Partition units.}
Row-holdout studies reserve 20\% of outer-training rows for validation.
Parkinsons instead partitions subjects at both levels, keeping every recording from a subject together; its recording count does not represent independent patients.
The additional grouped Airfoil experiment defines 106 conditions by exact tuples of angle, chord, velocity and suction-side displacement thickness, leaving frequency to vary within a condition.
The 5 shuffled folds test each condition once, with 20\% of outer-training conditions reserved for validation.
This tests held-out condition combinations, not necessarily unseen individual feature values.
Its partitions and training proportions differ from the row-holdout study, so their results are descriptive comparisons rather than a paired grouping effect.

\textbf{Constructed regression data.}
The generator draws independent $x_1,x_2\sim U(-2,2)$ and $z\sim N(0,1)$, then sets
\begin{align*}
\mu(x)&=\sin(x_1)+x_2^2,\\
y&=\mu(x)+\sigma(x)z,\\
\sigma_A(x)&=0.1+0.5|x_1|,\\
\sigma_B(x)&=0.6,\qquad \sigma_C(x)=2\sigma_A(x).
\end{align*}
Regime A provides input-dependent noise, B a constant-noise control, and C greater noise with the same spatial pattern as A.
A and B differ in both noise variation and overall variance.
The Gaussian generating law tests the chosen likelihood within its assumed distribution family.
Each of 10 fresh replicates contains 4{,}000 base observations, partitioned into 2{,}400 training, 800 validation and 800 test rows per regime.
Inputs, standard-normal draws and partitions are shared across regimes within a replicate, and model seeds are shared across variants and regimes.
Only $x_1,x_2$ enter the models; true means, scales and noise draws are evaluation-only.
An earlier constructed-data study used one draw rather than independent replicates, so its results are not pooled with the ten-replicate summaries reported here.
Figure~\ref{fig:new-data-snippets} shows genuine prepared values; Appendix~\ref{app:new-data-provenance} gives seed provenance and further examples.

\textbf{Quality and evaluation protocol.}
Preparation checks finite model values, excludes identifiers from features, freezes split indices and records source and processed-file checksums.
No scaling, imputation or outcome-based row filtering is applied, and source duplicates are retained.
Full Superconductivity contains 66 exact duplicate extra rows and 6{,}093 repeated-feature extra rows.
Among the distinct feature tuples, 2{,}374 carry more than one target value, spanning 8{,}423 rows.
Such tuples can cross row partitions, so the results do not show generalisation to unseen materials.
New-data runs retain the learner, score, learning rate and 2{,}000-stage budget from Section~\ref{sec:replication}, select stages by validation NLL, and refit on training plus validation rows.
For Homoscedastic, each stage's scale is the RMS of the validation residuals and the selected scale is retained after refitting, unlike the released-code calculation in Section~\ref{sec:replication}.
This comparator uses validation outcomes for both scale estimation and stage selection; the other variants use them only for stage selection.
Regression targets come from the public sources or generator; only the authored explanations evaluated in Section~\ref{sec:new-results} receive human labels.
